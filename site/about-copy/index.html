Staff & Faculty

Here at IBA we have an experienced and passionate teaching staff. Every day our students are taught from a total of 170 years of experience just from our full-time teachers. Every one of our full-time teachers has his/her degree in teaching. We have several part-time teachers who have degrees in various fields. You will find our staff friendly, professional and dedicated to your students.

A foundation today for success tomorrow!
