Transcript Requests

Current students and alumni can request a transcript here. Please allow at least 3 business days for processing.

  • Official transcripts go straight from the school to the college, employer or other recipient (by mail or secure email).
  • Unofficial copies are for your own records.
  • There is a transcript fee. We send the transcript once it is paid.
  • Good financial standing: we can only release a transcript when the student's account is in good financial standing with the school. Any past-due balance must be paid first.

Questions? Call (301) 856-1616 or email iba@ibcministries.org.
