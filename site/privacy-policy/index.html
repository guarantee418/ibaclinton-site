Privacy Policy

Last updated October 2026.

This website is run by Independent Baptist Academy, a ministry of Independent Baptist Church, 9255 Piscataway Rd, Clinton, MD 20735. This page explains what information the site collects and how it is used.

Information you send us

When you fill out the Request Info form, we receive the details you enter (such as your name, email, phone number and information about your student). The form is sent to our school office through the school’s own Microsoft 365 account, and we email you a confirmation. We use this information only to answer your request and help you with enrollment. We do not sell or share it.

When you sign up for a school tour or a parent-teacher conference, order hot lunch or request a transcript on this site, the details you enter are saved for our school office in the school’s own Microsoft 365 account, and we email you a confirmation. We use them only for that purpose.

When you report a student absence, the details are saved for our school office in the same account, and we email you a confirmation and tell your child’s homeroom teacher. When you send feedback to the administration from The Kings Corner, what you write (and your name and contact details, if you choose to give them) is saved for our school office in the same account. No confirmation email is sent.

Outside services

Some links and pages use outside services with their own privacy policies: Microsoft Forms (camp registration), Gradelink (parent portal), Anedot (online giving) and Facebook. When you use those services, their privacy policies apply.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. Google Analytics sets cookies to do this; it does not tell us who you are. The site does not use advertising cookies.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy, or would like us to delete information you sent us, contact the school office at (301) 856-1616 or iba@ibcministries.org.
